ⓘ How the meta score is calculated
The score (roughly 0–100) is built in three tiers:
• Top ~20 by live usage — pinned to the current Pikalytics Reg M-B ranking, with hand-set S/A/B tiers (scores ~76–95). This is what's actually winning right now.
• ~42 established meta mons — usage-anchored scores from real competitive data.
• Everyone else — a formula from base-stat total + defensive typing: clamp(20–60, (BST−380)/220×30 + 28 + typing×0.7 + 3 if Mega). Outside the top ~60 this reflects raw strength/bulk, not live usage — so treat lower scores as "how good on paper," not "how popular."

🌦️ Weather & field extenders

  • Light Clay — screens & Aurora Veil last 8 turns.
  • Heat Rock / Damp Rock / Smooth Rock / Icy Rock — sun / rain / sand / snow last 8 turns.
  • Great on your weather setter to keep the field up longer.

💥 Damage & survival staples

  • Life Orb — +30% damage (10% recoil).
  • Type-boost items (Charcoal, Magnet…) — +20% to one type.
  • Choice Scarf — +50% Speed, locked into one move (the only Choice item here).
  • Focus Sash — survive one hit from full HP; Sitrus Berry heals 25%; resist berries halve a super-effective hit.
